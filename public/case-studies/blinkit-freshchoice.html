01 / PROBLEM STATEMENT

In date doesn’t always mean enough time.

Customers can receive groceries with too little remaining shelf life for their planned use. Discovering the date only after delivery can mean rushed consumption, another purchase or wasted food.

Someone using a pack tomorrow may welcome a discount; someone stocking up may prefer to keep their original selection. Today’s uncertainty is the opportunity FreshChoice addresses.

Origin: the author’s firsthand concern. Wider prevalence and customer behavior still need validation.

02 / USER STORY

Let me choose what fits my plans.

As a customer buying groceries I’ll use soon, I want to see a discounted pack’s exact date before choosing it, so I can save money without a shelf-life surprise.

Success means: I understand the date and saving, can decline or undo easily, and receive the product and date promise I accepted.

03 / Experience and interactive screens

A small choice inside Blinkit checkout.

FreshChoice appears below the basket items only when a suitable batch is available. A lightweight review sheet explains the tradeoff; the original pack stays selected by default.

  1. Review: see the same product, printed date and saving.
  2. Choose: explicitly select and apply FreshChoice.
  3. Confirm: review the updated total; undo before payment.

Tap the green controls or choose a screen above. This is a local demo with sample data; it places no order and takes no payment.

Blinkit checkout patterns are reconstructed from public references. FreshChoice is a proposed addition; exact app-version parity is not claimed.

MVP: sealed, dated dairy, bakery and egg packs of the same product and quantity. No loose or undated stock, other-brand swaps or automatic enrollment in future orders.

04 / Essential product requirements

What must work.

RequirementExpected behavior
Eligible offers onlyRecommend the same SKU and full line quantity only when batch dates, stock, storage and approved sale rules are verified.
Clear, voluntary choiceShow the exact printed date, current price and discounted price. Opening or closing the sheet changes nothing; apply requires explicit consent.
Accurate basketReserve the chosen batch, recalculate charges and show the final total before payment. Undo releases the hold and restores the validated original.
Correct physical packTrack stock by batch. Use FEFO within eligible, consented stock; verify the SKU, batch and pack date at picking.
Fair recoveryIf unavailable before payment, return to a clearly priced original basket. After payment, offer the same item with an equal or later date at no extra charge, or refund the affected item. Never silently downgrade.

05 / Success measures

Trust, usefulness and business value.

Measure offer acceptance, accurate date fulfillment, checkout completion, added picking time and date or quality complaints. Test whether lower write-offs create positive contribution after discounts, labor, refunds and lost full-price margin.

These are proposed measures, not achieved results. Validate customer understanding and batch accuracy in a small pilot before expanding.

06 / Safety and feasibility

A discount cannot make a batch eligible.

Unknown dates, recalls, damaged packs or storage failures suppress the offer. Recheck eligibility against expected delivery time. Launch depends on reliable batch capture, reservation, pick-scan validation and approved category rules.

Indian food and labeling requirements need Food Safety and Legal sign-off. Customer consent never overrides safety rules or statutory rights.
