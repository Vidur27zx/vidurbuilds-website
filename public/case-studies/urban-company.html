Recovering an unresolved
home-service booking

A proposed recovery flow that connects AI support, customer choice and operational ownership when a service stays in progress.

ScopeProduct management · UX · Service recovery
EvidenceTwo firsthand experiences · Secunderabad
ContributionProblem framing, decisions, flow and MVP
StatusIndependent concept · Not validated

ProblemAn active booking and repeated support responses left me without a clear next action when service commitments slipped.

RecommendationGive customers a choice when a service slips: accept a workable date, request another professional, request cancellation review or reach customer support. Keep one recovery case with an accountable owner.

01 / Context and evidence

A visible status without a usable next step

Firsthand account · Carpenter

Sunday → Monday → Tuesday → Wednesday

The booking was repeatedly rescheduled. The carpenter was not answering my calls, while the booking appeared active or in progress beyond the expected time. I needed an update or a way to cancel; the AI support flow repeated without connecting me to a human during those attempts.

Completed after a week of uncertainty

The washing machine repair was completed after approximately one week, but the delay was compounded by difficulty reaching the professional and getting updates. 02 / Product judgment

Prioritize a recovery path that operations can deliver

Target user: a customer whose active booking has missed a commitment and who needs an update, a different professional or cancellation review. Numerical prioritization would be premature without reach and effort data.

DecisionWhy it earns priorityTrade-off or boundary
P0 · One continuing casePreserves booking history, the customer’s latest acceptable time and ownership across repeat contacts.Reuse an existing case system if it already supports this; avoid a parallel queue.
P0 · Verified recovery choicesTurns a delayed status into an actionable request.Reassignment needs supply checks. Cancellation after partial work may require review.
P0 · Direct human handoffGives customers an exit when automation cannot resolve the issue.Requires queue capacity and a realistic update commitment.
P1 · Proactive alertsMay help customers before they need to seek support.Add after validating commitment timestamps and exception accuracy.
Defer · Predictive delays and compensationNot necessary to test whether accountable recovery improves the experience.Needs reliable labels, approved policies and greater operational maturity.
Design principle: every unresolved request should have a visible next step, an owning team and a commitment the service can keep.

04 / MVP requirements and service design

Make the flow operationally complete

MVP objective: help eligible customers recover within their available time, with fewer repeated attempts. Pilot a bounded carpenter-booking cohort in Secunderabad, subject to sufficient volume and staffed support.

RequirementAcceptance criteriaProposed accountability
Accurate statusDistinguish customer-reported from verified delays; show the latest verified event and timestamp. Missing data never becomes an invented ETA.Engineering: booking event integrity.
Safe requests and actionsRequire customer consent to a new date. Capture the latest acceptable completion time; check policy, partial work, payment, parts and supply. Show any applicable charges before confirmation. Reconcile uncertain action results before retrying.Engineering and operations: eligibility and action safety.
Persistent caseRepeat entry returns the linked open case. Preserve intent, known facts, attempted actions and unknowns; restrict access to relevant staff.Support operations: ownership and continuity.
Reliable follow-throughExpose owning team and a capacity-backed update time. Escalate missed deadlines. Record operational completion separately from customer-confirmed resolution; allow reopening.Support owner and queue supervisor.

Compact service blueprint

StageCustomer-facing behaviorBackstage action
DetectVisible delay or customer issue report.Validate commitment events and professional status.
DecideEligible recovery choices and clear consequences.Check policy, work performed, parts readiness, replacement capacity and the customer’s latest acceptable time.
RecoverCase tracker with ownership and update time.Agent coordinates dispatch or specialist review; AI transfers verified context.
CloseAction receipt, outcome check and reopen route.Reconcile booking, payment and case records. Silence is not customer confirmation.

AI → human rules

Honor an explicit human request immediately. Offer handoff after two unsuccessful attempts on the same intent; validate that threshold in the pilot. Send partial-work, payment disputes and conflicting data to an authorized person. Safety-related reports follow the approved urgent procedure.

Repeated postponement rule: a second reschedule request, or a proposed date beyond the customer’s latest acceptable time, triggers operations review. This threshold is a proposal to validate. AI summarizes verified facts and explains options. It cannot invent arrival times, availability, policy entitlements or completed actions.

States that prevent dead ends

Loading fails: disclose unverified status and allow a review request. No replacement within the customer’s time: say so explicitly; offer cancellation review or customer support without silently extending the deadline. Agent unavailable: preserve the case and show the staffed route. Update missed: acknowledge the breach and escalate ownership.

Release gate: do not promise timed updates until an accountable team can meet them. Verify keyboard access, status announcements and readable contrast.

05 / Validation and next steps

Measure an outcome beyond the conversation

MeasureDefinitionDecision use
Primary · Confirmed resolutionEligible episodes with verified recovery and customer confirmation within a predefined window ÷ all eligible episodes.Report nonresponse separately; retain unresolved episodes in the denominator.
Customer effortTime to actionable recovery, resolution within the customer’s accepted time, and same-issue repeat contacts within seven days.Check whether recovery improves beyond acknowledgment.
GuardrailsQueue age, agent minutes per episode, missed updates, reopened cases and unsupported promises or unauthorized changes.Investigate deterioration; pause the affected path after an unauthorized booking or payment action.

Validate first: reconcile incident records and review existing routes with support. Test the flow with 6–8 relevant customers for comprehension of options, request status and ownership. Run historical cases in shadow mode before exposing live actions.

Then pilot: compare a bounded cohort against existing approved support while preserving access to help. Establish baseline, sample size, minimum meaningful effect and stopping rules before launch; account for shared-queue spillover. Track case events from exception through owner acceptance, action completion, outcome confirmation and reopening.

Roadmap: validate records and operations → pilot agent-reviewed recovery → add proactive alerts → extend to washing machine repair after category policy review. Automate eligible actions only after event quality and staffing reliability are demonstrated.

Recommendation and limits

Prioritize a continuing recovery case, valid customer choices and a reliable human handoff. If the capability already exists, focus on discoverability and promise reliability instead of introducing a duplicate system.

No interviews, usability sessions or experiments have been completed for this proposal. No measured uplift is claimed. Two recalled experiences are a starting point for investigation; internal causes, broader incidence and delivery effort remain unknown.
