01 / THE PROBLEM

Promotions and delivery codes shouldn't compete.

In India, shopping updates, offers, and ordinary conversations often arrive in the same WhatsApp inbox. Blocking a brand may stop promotions — but it can also interrupt useful updates from that same business.

“I get 7–10 promotional messages in a day, but I still need to find my Amazon or Flipkart delivery code.”

04 / SYSTEM DESIGN

Use existing template categories.

Instead of analyzing every private message with AI, the proposed system starts with existing approved business-template categories and a small set of inbox-state rules.

EXISTINGBusiness creates a templateMarketing · Utility · Authentication
EXISTINGMeta reviews categoryApproval and pricing classification
PROPOSEDConsumer inbox routingMessage metadata + active conversation state
INBOX OUTCOMES
ChatsUtility, Authentication, service, personal, unknown
PromotionsApproved Marketing template messages
Active stateReply-triggered conversation; 7-day lifecycle
Flows and click-to-WhatsApp ads are not message categories.

A WhatsApp Flow is an interactive experience; the approved template used to send it may be Marketing or Utility. A customer opening a conversation from an Instagram or Facebook ad is not automatically a Marketing-template message. In the MVP, free-form replies without trustworthy classification stay in Chats.

THE CRITICAL SAFETY DECISION

Separate delivery updates from discounts.

Today, templates containing both utility and promotional material can be classified as Marketing. Our proposal requires Meta to enforce clearer separation before approval. This is a new policy dependency, not an existing guaranteed safeguard.

Launch blocker until mixed-template safety is validated.
01Unknown stays visible

No category? No automatic hiding.

02One original conversation

03Marketing doesn't refresh activity

Only meaningful interactions reset the 7-day window.

04Nothing is deleted

Search, access, and cleanup undo remain available.

02 / DELIVERY SCOPE

MVP includes

  • Promotions filter and clean Chats view
  • Automatic template-category routing
  • One conversation with two entry points
  • Customizable promotional notifications
  • 7-day active-conversation lifecycle
  • Opt-in beta, controlled rollout
  • Optional metadata-backed historical cleanup

Not in V1

  • AI content inspection and classification
  • SMS categorization
  • Blocking or deleting messages
  • Personalized offers or recommendations
  • Entire business-account labeling
  • Automatic sorting of free-form replies
06 / TRADE-OFFS & FEASIBILITY

Risks and feasibility.

The interface can be designed quickly. Production readiness depends on internal message metadata, template-policy enforcement, and a migration path that doesn't surprise users.

High feasibility

UI and conversation state

New filter, message-level views, notification controls, and seven-day active-state logic are standard product engineering patterns.

Platform dependency

Consumer metadata access

Business categories exist, but category data must be reliably available to the consumer client for routing.

Release blocker

Mixed-template safety

Critical transactional content inside Marketing templates must be prevented or safely handled before broad deployment.

THE PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

Less clutter.
Same conversations.

Start with business template categories Meta already uses. Keep Utility and Authentication messages visible. Preserve one original thread, even when the message appears in different inbox views.
